# arXiv Submission Metadata

## Title
Task-Appropriate Hybrid Architectures: A Unified Theory of Gain Across Metrics, Architectures, and Tasks

## Authors
Erdem Esa (Independent Researcher)

## Abstract
Hybrid systems do not win unconditionally: their gain is a function of
metric type, architecture type, and task structure. We provide an exact
identity for linear metrics and a per-class extension for macro F1,
which we show is necessary because the linear weight does not collapse
for non-linear metrics. We further show that Kronecker architectures
are 8x--12.6x more parameter-efficient on grid-structured, noisy tasks;
the advantage disappears on graph tasks and on exact bilinear tasks
(optimisation-limited), and reverses under clean data. Kronecker's
benefit is therefore conditional on both structural alignment and
regularisation need. These results suggest that hybrid system design
can be made principled: formula-driven for metrics, structure-driven
for tasks.
