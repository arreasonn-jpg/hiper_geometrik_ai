\begin{abstract}
We describe an experimental research platform built around residual bilinear
matrix sandwiches $A X B$ and a fixed-size hash-addressed memory. The work is
presented with a strict distinction between physical trainable parameters,
interaction upper bounds, and conceptual memory-address upper bounds. We
provide source-hash-verified Turkish and English dependency-arc verification
protocols, parameter-controlled architecture baselines, and experiment
manifests. This is an artifact draft, not a claim of general language ability,
scaling-law identification, or a peer-reviewed result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and accounting}
The default configuration contains 6,970,433 dense parameters and 33,554,432
physically allocated sparse-table parameters. These quantities are physical
parameters. They must not be conflated with the interaction upper bound
$C_I^{UB}$ or conceptual memory address upper bound $C_M^{UB}$.

\section{Bilinear chain theory}
For one layer, vectorization gives
\[
 \operatorname{vec}(A X B) = (B^\top \otimes A)\operatorname{vec}(X).
\]
Therefore $\operatorname{rank}(B^\top\otimes A)=\operatorname{rank}(A)
\operatorname{rank}(B)$. A generic full-rank factor pair can yield rank $n^2$,
but the Kronecker-structured family does not equal the unrestricted
$n^2\times n^2$ matrix family. Its generic single-layer manifold dimension is
at most $2n^2-1$, accounting for the reciprocal scaling symmetry of the two
factors.

Without nonlinearity, depth collapses exactly:
\[
 A_2(A_1 X B_1)B_2=(A_2A_1)X(B_1B_2).
\]
Thus a claim that $n^{2K}$ is realized linear capacity is invalid. In the
implemented model, SiLU, LayerNorm, and residual connections prevent this
linear collapse; that fact alone is not a universality or generalization proof.
A closed-form VC or pseudo-dimension for the full trainable SiLU/LayerNorm HGA
chain is not established here. The only stated VC result concerns a fixed
$n^2$-dimensional representation with an affine binary readout, whose VC
upper bound is $n^2+1$.

\section{Reproducible benchmarks}
The Turkish protocol uses a pinned Turkish Web Treebank revision
\texttt{40838e5cbe3f2882d4e768a3d782e6219e50b52a}; vendored CoNLL-U source
files are SHA-256 verified. The English protocol uses pinned UD English EWT
revision \texttt{4a4d77f599ea53cc405f85d0cec4b2f14f81d42b}; all official
source split files and their CC-BY-SA-4.0 provenance are vendored and
verified. Both protocols formulate the narrow task of distinguishing an
annotated basic dependency arc from a deterministic same-sentence corrupted
head.

On a deterministic English EWT subset, five seeds compare dense, vanilla
Transformer, BERT-style bidirectional, and GPT-style causal controls. The
BERT-style and GPT-style models are small random-initialized models trained
from scratch; they are not pretrained BERT/GPT checkpoints. All reported
artifacts include source hashes, candidate hashes, seed-level values, means,
and population standard deviations.

\section{Scaling}
A three-size HGA English EWT parameter probe is included as a diagnostic. Its
parameter range is only 3.28$\times$ and it holds the task and short training
schedule fixed. It is explicitly insufficient to identify a scaling law or to
extrapolate to foundation-model behavior.

\section{Limitations}
The evidence is bilingual rather than broad multilingual coverage, is not an
end-to-end language-model evaluation, and contains no pretrained LLM baseline.
External submission, authorship confirmation, independent review, and
peer-review outcomes are outside the capabilities of this source repository.
